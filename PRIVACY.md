# Sprig privacy policy

_Last updated: October 16, 2026_

Sprig is a mind-mapping app. Each Sprig server is run privately by its owner for themselves and a short list of invited people. This policy explains what a Sprig server stores when you sign in.

## What Sprig stores

- **Your account:** the email address your sign-in provider (Google, Facebook or X) confirms, your display name, and your profile picture address.
- **Sign-in links:** for each provider you connect, the provider's account ID for you and the email it reported. Sprig never sees or stores your provider password.
- **Sessions:** a hashed sign-in token, when it was created and last used, and the browser and IP address it came from. Sessions end when you sign out or after 30 days without use.
- **Your maps:** the maps you create, older versions of them for undo-style recovery (kept up to 90 days), and maps you delete (kept for 30 days in Recently deleted, then removed).
- **Share links:** if you share a map, a hashed and encrypted copy of the link. Sprig records nothing about people who open a shared link.

## What Sprig does with it

Sprig uses this information only to sign you in, keep you signed in, and store and show your maps. It does not sell, rent or share your information. It runs no advertising and no third-party analytics. Sign-in requests go to the provider you choose (Google, Facebook or X), under that provider's own privacy policy.

## Your choices

- **Download all your maps** at any time from the account menu.
- **Disconnect** a sign-in provider from the account menu, as long as one other remains connected.
- To have your account and maps deleted, ask the owner of the Sprig server you use. For the project itself, open an issue at <https://github.com/skarlekar/sprig/issues>.
